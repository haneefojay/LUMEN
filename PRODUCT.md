# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js 15 (React 19), React Three Fiber (R3F), Three.js, @react-three/drei, GSAP + ScrollTrigger, Framer Motion, Tailwind CSS, Lucide React, Google Fonts (Cormorant Garamond, Inter Tight, JetBrains Mono).

## Users

Design aficionados, creative technologists, 3D artists, Awwwards jury, digital art curators and collectors seeking an awe-inspiring, museum-grade spatial experience on the web.

## Product Purpose

LUMEN is a digital atelier and virtual museum that exhibits "impossible objects" — procedural and shader-driven 3D artifacts that violate physical laws (non-orientable topology, continuous fluid metamorphism, shattered gravity, geometric hyper-crystallization).

## Positioning

Unlike conventional corporate 3D showcases or tech landing pages, LUMEN is an uncompromising digital institution. It does not sell subscriptions or pitch features; it invites visitors across a spatial narrative threshold into the philosophical contemplation of impossible digital matter.

## Operating Context

Desktop and mobile modern web browsers (Chrome, Edge, Safari, Firefox) with WebGL2 support. Operates as a seamless single-page continuous scroll narrative with horizontal gallery pan, interactive object manipulation, material wireframe inspection, and colophon.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- High-performance procedural geometries & custom WebGL shaders running at 60fps.
- Camera choreography synced seamlessly between DOM scroll (GSAP ScrollTrigger) and R3F scene graph.
- Progressive degradation for low-power or mobile environments.
- Zero tolerance for AI-slop tropes: no centered generic hero grids, no purple gradients, no default Inter font, no standard ease-in-out curves.

## Brand Commitments

- **Name:** LUMEN
- **Tagline:** "Objects that should not exist."
- **Tone:** Mysterious, confident, contemplative, prestigious, museum-grade.
- **Palette:** Near-black background (`#0A0A0C`), warm bone accent (`#E8D5B7`), cool slate depth (`#6C7A89`), muted gold highlights (`#C9A96E`).
- **Typography:** Cormorant Garamond (Display Serif), Inter Tight (Clean geometric body), JetBrains Mono (Technical telemetry & museum registry).

## Evidence on Hand

- Complete concept brief detailing 5 narrative acts: Hero "The Threshold", Manifesto, Exhibit Hall (4 impossible objects), The Studio (craft & shader reveal), Colophon / Footer.
- Motionsites research notes & motion parameters.

## Product Principles

1. **Spatial Authenticity:** Every visual element belongs to the architectural space of the virtual museum.
2. **Weight & Deliberation:** Motion is physical, heavy, and majestic — using custom spring and exponential cubic-beziers.
3. **Typography as Architecture:** Monumental scale contrast, clip-path reveals, and precise technical annotations.
4. **Zero Slop:** Strict compliance with Impeccable and modern web craft standards.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Semantic HTML landmark elements (`header`, `main`, `section`, `footer`).
- `prefers-reduced-motion` media query support with graceful static fallbacks.
- Accessible ARIA labels and keyboard navigation for exhibit exploration.
- High contrast bone/gold text on deep black background (contrast ratio > 12:1).
