# LUMEN taste profile

A local, auditable taste profile assembled from public award-gallery references because no Taste integration is connected in this session.

## References and extracted vocabulary
1. [Noomo Labs — immersive 3D hero](https://www.awwwards.com/inspiration/immersive-3d-hero-section-noomo-labs) — central object as environment; typography remains a separate depth plane.
2. [Salt & Pepper — interactive WebGL particles](https://www.awwwards.com/inspiration/interactive-webgl-particles-on-hero-image-salt-and-pepper) — particles describe interaction rather than fill empty space.
3. [Héroïne — 3D temple hero](https://www.awwwards.com/inspiration/3d-temple-hero-image-heroine) — monumental crop and camera-led reveal.
4. [CRZ Studio — WebGL hero](https://www.awwwards.com/inspiration/webgl-hero-crz-studio) — full-viewport singular visual hierarchy.
5. [Visual Identity Studio — interactive typography](https://www.awwwards.com/inspiration/interactive-hero-typography-visual-identity-studio) — type behaves as an object, not a caption.
6. [Wanaka — typography hero](https://www.awwwards.com/inspiration/expertise-hero-wanaka) — controlled contrast between type scale and small navigation.
7. [Cult of the North — WebGL scroll experience](https://www.awwwards.com/inspiration/webgl-scroll-experience-cult-of-the-north) — scroll is a camera transport, not a reveal queue.
8. [evensix — website intro](https://www.awwwards.com/inspiration/website-intro-evensix) — intro, page transition, and WebGL share one motion grammar.
9. [Stripe Sessions 2023 — hero title](https://www.awwwards.com/inspiration/hero-title-stripe-sessions-2023) — noise, type, and depth are composed as one field.
10. [Luis Bizarro](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/luis-bizarro) — real-time 3D can remain legible with a nearly binary palette.
11. [Lusion Labs](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/lusion-labs) — projects become navigation through direct manipulation.
12. [The Watch](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/the-watch) — luxury comes from lighting and material restraint, not extra interface chrome.
13. [Vredestein New Horizons](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/vredestein-new-horizons) — the museum metaphor is strongest when navigation has spatial consequence.
14. [Museum of Annoying Experiences](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/museum-of-annoying-experiences) — exhibition framing can carry wit without weakening authority.
15. [Joseph Santamaria 3D Portfolio](https://www.webgpu.com/showcase/joseph-santamaria-3d-webgl-portfolio) — hand-composed camera paths and spatial typography make the page feel like a place.

## Profile
- **Composition:** asymmetric, edge-cropped, one dominant form per viewport.
- **Typography:** extreme scale contrast; serif as atmosphere, mono as catalogue logic.
- **Material:** rough mineral, ceramic, smoke, diffraction; never glossy toy plastic.
- **Motion:** long narrative arcs, short feedback; camera transport over stacked reveal effects.
- **Interface:** almost absent; hairlines and coordinates instead of cards.
- **Color:** warm bone in a near-black conservation chamber; gold only on contact.
- **Avoid:** neon tech glow, centered launch-page composition, ornamental blur, glass card stacks, random floating geometry.
