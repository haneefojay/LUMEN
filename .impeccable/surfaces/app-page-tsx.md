# Home surface brief

## Scope and mode
Single-page home exhibition. Visitor mode: Experience.

## Audience, job, action, proof
Design-conscious visitors should understand within seconds that LUMEN exhibits impossible digital matter, then scroll to inspect four artifacts. The only first-viewport action is scrolling. Proof is the experience itself: responsive procedural objects, coherent spatial transitions, and a transparent studio section.

## Constraints
Readable without WebGL; reduced-motion and low-power modes; no invented clients or awards; no generic landing-page patterns.

## Direction contract
**THESIS:** The browser is a conservation chamber for matter that cannot survive outside computation. Refuse the centered marketing hero and the sequence of boxed sections.

**OWN-WORLD:** Warm bone type and mineral surfaces hover in a near-black void. Slate recedes; muted gold appears only under contact. Hairlines, catalogue numbers, and technical mono create museum authority without imitating a newspaper.

**STORY:** Cross a threshold, encounter a manifesto, move laterally through four impossible objects, inspect the studio’s construction logic, and leave on a quiet final pulse.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** A monumental seeded artifact occupies the right two-thirds and crosses the crop. LUMEN begins low-left, partially occluded and revealed by scroll. A tiny vertical index sits at upper-left; the scroll trace rests near the lower edge. No button.

**FORM:** Code-led immersive scroll narrative, selected directly because the user supplied a complete world and site structure. Signature interaction: one camera journey binds threshold, hall, and colophon.

**FINISH:** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
